Privacy Policy

Last updated: May 22, 2026

Yardr ("we", "our", or "us") is a mobile app that helps people find and host garage sales. This Privacy Policy explains what information we collect, how we use it, and your rights.

Information We Collect

  • Account information: When you sign in with Apple or Google, we receive your name and email address.
  • Location: We use your device location to show nearby sales and to geo-tag sales you post. Location is only accessed when you use the app.
  • Sale listings: Photos, descriptions, dates, and addresses you submit when posting a sale.
  • Wishlist items: Items you save to your wishlist so we can notify you of matching sales.
  • Push notification token: Used to send you reminders and wishlist match alerts. Cleared when you log out.
  • Phone number: Optionally provided on your seller profile so buyers can contact you.

How We Use Your Information

  • To show you nearby garage sales based on your location
  • To publish sales you post so others can find them
  • To match your wishlist against new sale listings and notify you
  • To send reminders for sales you've saved
  • To let you sign in securely with Apple or Google

AI Features

When you post a sale, photos and item descriptions may be sent to an AI service (Anthropic Claude) to generate suggested tags. This data is used only to generate tags and is not stored or used for training.

Data Sharing

We do not sell your personal information. Sale listings (photos, description, address) are public by design — that's how buyers find you. We share data with the following third-party services to operate the app:

  • Supabase — database and authentication hosting
  • Expo / EAS — push notification delivery
  • Google Maps — map display and geocoding
  • Anthropic — AI tag generation
  • Apple / Google — sign-in authentication

Data Retention

Your account and listings are retained until you delete your account. You can delete your account at any time from the Settings screen in the app, which removes your profile and all associated data.

Children's Privacy

Yardr is not directed at children under 13. We do not knowingly collect information from children under 13.

Your Rights

You can request access to, correction of, or deletion of your personal data by contacting us or using the in-app account deletion feature.
